HOW CALIBRATION WORKS

Each reading is normalized to ISO 100 at full power to estimate a guide number. The app uses the median of readings for each beam, resisting isolated outliers. It does not model nonlinearity with distance or power.

Only calibrated modes are labelled measured. Until you measure W mode, its loss relative to N remains an assumption.

ABOUT THIS CALCULATOR

Flash-only exposure is calculated with GN = aperture × distance; power and ISO scale with their square roots. This assumes conventional direct flash and the film-speed exposure relationship. Roundoff is to the nearest ⅓ EV on the iT30Pro.

The Nikon FE uses conventional X-sync at 1/125 s or slower. There is no film TTL metering or HSS in this setup. This tool cannot meter reflected light or infer flash-to-subject distance automatically.
